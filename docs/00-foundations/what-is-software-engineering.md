## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Distinguish between coding, programming, and software engineering.
- Describe the activities that software engineers perform beyond writing code.
- Explain why engineering practices like testing and collaboration matter.

## Introduction

Software engineering is much more than writing code. It encompasses designing, testing, deploying, and maintaining software systems—while collaborating with teams and users throughout the process.
