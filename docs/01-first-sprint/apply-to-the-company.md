## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Complete a simulated job-application survey.
- Draft an initial resume that highlights relevant skills.
- Understand how technical hiring processes work at software companies.

## Introduction

Our course is structured as a simulated software engineering job. In this first section, you'll go through an application process, introducing yourself and creating your first resume.
