## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Explain what mutation testing is and why it matters.
- Interpret a mutation testing report.
- Improve test coverage to kill surviving mutants.

## Introduction

Mutation testing introduces small changes ('mutants') into your code to check whether your tests detect them. It's a powerful way to evaluate the quality of your test suite.
