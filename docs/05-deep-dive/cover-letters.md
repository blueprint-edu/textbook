**Topics:** Cover letter writing, Connecting experience to job requirements, Professional communication

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Write a cover letter tailored to a specific role.
- Connect your project experience to stated job requirements.
- Strike a professional and authentic tone.

## Introduction

A strong cover letter contextualizes your resume and demonstrates genuine interest in a role. It's an opportunity to tell your professional story in your own words.
