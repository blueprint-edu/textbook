## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Plan and conduct a usability test.
- Observe users without leading them.
- Report usability findings clearly.

## Introduction

Usability testing puts real users in front of your software. Their struggles and successes reveal design improvements that you'd never discover on your own.
