**Topics:** Technical interviews, Problem-solving under pressure, Communicating your thinking

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Work through an algorithmic problem aloud.
- Communicate reasoning clearly during a technical interview.
- Handle uncertainty gracefully when you don't know an answer.

## Introduction

Whiteboard interviews test problem-solving and communication. Even as companies evolve their hiring practices, being able to think out loud about technical problems is a valuable professional skill.
