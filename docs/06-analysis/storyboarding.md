**Topics:** Storyboards, Scenario-based design, More wireframes

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Create a storyboard to illustrate a user scenario.
- Use scenarios to reveal design gaps.
- Iterate on wireframes based on scenario analysis.

## Introduction

Storyboards tell the story of how a user accomplishes a goal with your software. They reveal context, emotion, and workflow that wireframes alone cannot capture.
