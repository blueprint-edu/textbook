## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Conduct a contextual inquiry session.
- Take effective field notes.
- Synthesize observations into design insights.

## Introduction

Contextual inquiry involves observing users in their natural environment. It reveals the gap between what users say they do and what they actually do.
