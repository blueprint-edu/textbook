## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Give constructive feedback in a team setting.
- Manage disagreement productively.
- Describe the characteristics of an effective team member.

## Introduction

Technical skills only go so far. Being a great team member—reliable, communicative, and supportive—is equally important for long-term career success.
