## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Participate in sprint planning.
- Estimate the effort required for user stories.
- Break large stories into smaller tasks.

## Introduction

Sprint planning turns the product backlog into a sprint plan. The team commits to a set of work and breaks it into concrete tasks.
