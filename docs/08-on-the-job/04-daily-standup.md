**Topics:** Daily standups, Status updates, Blockers

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Give a concise daily standup update.
- Identify and escalate blockers.
- Use standups to coordinate with teammates.

## Introduction

The daily standup is a 15-minute synchronization meeting. Done well, it surfaces blockers early and keeps the team aligned on daily progress.
