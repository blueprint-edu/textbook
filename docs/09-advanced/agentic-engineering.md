## Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

- Describe the risks introduced by agentic AI systems.
- Apply the 'do not trust' principle to AI-generated code.
- Design workflows that preserve human oversight.

## Introduction

Agentic engineering involves AI systems that take actions autonomously. This introduces new risks around trust, verification, and control that every engineer must understand.
